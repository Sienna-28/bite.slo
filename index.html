How Prices And Filters Work

Typical spending ranges estimate one person's meal before tax, tip, alcohol, and optional add-ons. Restaurant prices can change, so use each restaurant's official menu link for current pricing.

  • Under $15: a lower-cost meal or snack is commonly available.
  • $15–30: a typical casual lunch or dinner range.
  • $30+: a more elevated dinner, seafood, steak, or multi-course experience.

How We Choose Places

The directory focuses on established Central Coast restaurants. Entries are checked against restaurant websites and regional dining references before they are added.

  • Every listing includes a restaurant website or menu link.
  • Closed restaurants and unverified locations are excluded.
  • Campus labels are planning aids, not live route instructions.
  • Check current hours, reservations, menus, and specials before visiting.
