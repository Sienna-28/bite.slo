How Prices And Filters Work

Typical spending ranges estimate one person’s meal before tax, tip, alcohol, and optional add-ons. Restaurant prices change, so use each restaurant's official menu link for the current price.

  • Under $15: a lower-cost meal or snack is commonly available.
  • $15–30: a typical casual lunch or dinner range.
  • $30+: a more elevated dinner, seafood, steak, or multi-course experience.

How We Choose Places

The directory focuses on established restaurants in San Luis Obispo County that are useful to the local community, including San Luis Obispo, Morro Bay, Pismo Beach, Arroyo Grande, and Atascadero.

  • Every listing includes a restaurant website or menu link.
  • Campus travel labels are planning aids, not live route instructions.
