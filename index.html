How Prices And Filters Work

Price ranges estimate a typical individual meal. Filters describe the style, setting, cuisine, or general area of each restaurant and are intended to make choosing easier.

  • Under $15: a lower-cost meal, snack, or sweet is commonly available.
  • $15 to $30: a typical casual lunch or dinner range.
  • $30+: a more elevated dinner, seafood, steak, or multi-course experience.

How Places Are Listed

Bite SLO focuses on established restaurants and dessert shops in San Luis Obispo County. Each card links to a restaurant menu, ordering page, or official website whenever one is available.

  • Listings are organized by practical location, price, and dining style.
  • Campus lists are limited to approximately five miles of the named campus.
  • Check reservations, dietary needs, current menus, and special terms directly with the restaurant.
