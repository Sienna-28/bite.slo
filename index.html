Petra Mediterranean Pizza & Grill

Vegetarian friendly Group friendly Casual dinner

Thai Palace

Higuera Street, downtown SLO

A longtime local option for curries, noodles, stir-fries, and spice levels that can be adjusted to your table. Comfortable for lunch or dinner.

Date night Vegetarian friendly Downtown
