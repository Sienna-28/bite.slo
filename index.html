San Luis Obispo staples

Six reliable stops, from downtown patios to post-class counter service.

01 / COFFEE $

Scout Coffee

Morro Street, downtown SLO

A polished downtown coffee stop with house pastries, toast, and a calm, design-forward feel. Ideal for study sessions or a slow morning.

Coffee & casual Study spot Vegetarian options
Typical spend: $6–18 Menu & site
02 / AMERICAN $

Firestone Grill

Higuera Street, downtown SLO

The classic quick-service choice for tri-tip sandwiches, burgers, salads, and generous portions. Busy, casual, and easy with a group.

Family friendly Fast casual Central Coast classic
Typical spend: $12–22 View menu
03 / MEDITERRANEAN $

Petra Mediterranean Pizza & Grill

Higuera Street, downtown SLO

Build a meal around shawarma, kebabs, falafel, salads, and pizza. A flexible choice when your group has different diets and appetites.

Vegetarian friendly Group friendly Casual dinner
Typical spend: $13–25 View menu
04 / THAI $$

Thai Palace

Higuera Street, downtown SLO

A longtime local option for curries, noodles, stir-fries, and spice levels that can be adjusted to your table. Comfortable for lunch or dinner.

Date night Vegetarian friendly Downtown
Typical spend: $15–28 Menu & site
05 / CALIFORNIA $$

Novo Restaurant & Lounge

Higuera Street, downtown SLO

Seasonal California cooking in one of downtown's most distinctive settings, with creekside seating and a broad menu for lunch, dinner, and drinks.

Date night Patio Family friendly
Typical spend: $22–45 View menus
06 / JAPANESE $$

Goshi Japanese Restaurant

Monterey Street, downtown SLO

A respected local sushi and Japanese restaurant with nigiri, rolls, cooked dishes, and a more intimate dinner atmosphere.

Date night Sushi Reservations helpful
Typical spend: $25–50 Menu & site
